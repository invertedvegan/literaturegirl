Literature Girl

Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio, where I share drawings I have made.

I am self-taught. I make traditional art, and mainly draw animal commissions.

My preferred mediums are graphite, coloured pencils, pen and ink, and watercolours. I also like printmaking (intaglio especially), but this requires resources I don't usually have access to.

'Cats', (2024)

A laid-back sketch that I made to go alongside a letter I sent to an American friend. I was trying to have some fun with colour.

'Church Fundraiser Commission #3', (2024)

Another dog that I drew for somebody to raise some more money for my church.

'Church Fundraiser Commission #2', (2024)

Another drawing I made for a church parishioner as part of my fundraising effort. I don't love this drawing because it was difficult to find a way to integrate the grey fur, but I do like the eyes. I think I did those pretty well.

'Church Fundraiser Commission #1', (2024)

Something I was nervous about drawing, because I'd never drawn a cow before. It is a drawing of one of my minister's favourite cows. It was quite difficult to draw because there was so much white space and it needed to be on a white background, but I did my best.

'Odin', (2024)

My first ever attempt at a coloured pencil drawing. I asked my classmates for some photographs of their animals to use as references, because I want to start drawing more again, and I liked this one the most. I decided to try coloured pencils whenever my test sketch in graphite didn't seem to translate very well. I'm not very proud of it, and I wasn't really sure how to shade in the white areas, but I'm glad I tried coloured pencil out instead of letting it continue to scare me.

'Haskell', (2021)

Standard graphite pencil drawing on paper. A drawing of a Maine Coon an old friend used to own. This was my first ever attempt at drawing fur properly, and as a result I think I was too timid to add the contrast that I should have, but I'm still pretty happy with the cat's face and ears. The photograph provided is not of its fully finished state.

'Stag', (2021)

A birthday gift that I gave to my brother, who has a fondness for deer. The white space on the antlers is meant to signify snow, but it doesn't translate well without a background, which I didn't want to add. Fur is hard to draw.

'Heidi', (2023)

A Valentine's gift that I framed. This photograph was taken a while before I finished it completely. The perspective in the photograph that I worked from for this was rather strange, and certainly not optimal, but I liked how expressive the eyes were in it, so I stuck with it anyway.

'Drypoint', (2022)

Something I made whilst testing out drypoint etching, which I found I really enjoyed. Printmaking in general is a lot of fun, but I preferred this to lino cutting and monoprinting. To produce this piece, I etched the reversed image into plastic, applied black ink into the grooves produced, and then I pressed the sheet of plastic against damp watercolour paper.

'Brody', (2023)

A small, quick, sketchy drawing of my cousin's recently deceased dog. A surprise Christmas gift.
